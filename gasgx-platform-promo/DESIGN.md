# Design System

## Overview

GasGx OS is positioned as a control layer for distributed gas-power operations. The visual language is dark enterprise software with green system signals, blue diagnostic panels, monospace telemetry, and restrained red alerts. The promo should feel operational, intelligent, and dependable.

## Colors

- **Canvas**: `#08080D` — deep software-console background.
- **Panel**: `#12121A` — dashboard surfaces and cards.
- **Primary Text**: `#E2E8F0` — enterprise UI headings.
- **Muted Text**: `#9CA3AF` — supporting copy and metadata.
- **Accent Green**: `#5DD62C` — healthy state, active systems, CTA.
- **Diagnostic Blue**: `#1E5A75` — telemetry, edge links, and cloud layers.
- **Alert Red**: `#EF4444` — high-priority maintenance warnings only.

## Typography

- **Primary**: Inter, weights 400-900.
- **Telemetry**: JetBrains Mono, weights 400 and 700.
- **Display**: Inter 800, normal letter spacing for rendered video.

## Motion

- Use dashboard zooms, data sweeps, signal pulses, and panel-to-panel transitions.
- Green marks indicate healthy systems; red is reserved for a maintenance alert.
- Keep the composition dense but readable, with captions in a safe lower band.

## What Not To Do

- Do not turn the platform into a generic SaaS landing page.
- Do not invent AI capabilities or metrics beyond the captured page.
- Do not animate every dashboard element at once; keep one focal motion per beat.
- Do not let captions cover the primary dashboard cards.
