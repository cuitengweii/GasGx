# Design System

## Overview

GasGx G1000 is presented as a premium industrial power reveal. The product is intentionally hidden behind a dark curtain, so the promo should build anticipation through controlled light, silhouette, steel-blue edge glow, and GasGx green signals. The tone is engineered, cinematic, and restrained.

## Colors

- **Canvas**: `#050505` — black reveal stage.
- **Panel**: `#0F0F0F` — technical cards and CTA surfaces.
- **Primary Text**: `#FFFFFF` — product name and reveal claims.
- **Muted Text**: `#CBD5E1` — supporting copy.
- **Quiet Text**: `#94A3B8` — labels and metadata.
- **Accent Green**: `#5DD62C` — GasGx identity, CTA, and system signal.
- **Reveal Blue**: `#1E5A75` — restrained edge light taken from the captured product visual.

## Typography

- **Primary**: Inter, weights 300-900.
- **Technical labels**: Inter 700 with normal letter spacing.
- **Product display**: Inter 900, large but controlled.

## Motion

- Use curtain-like wipes, slow product push-ins, scanning lights, and measured reveals.
- Keep anticipation longer than the CTA; this product page is explicitly a teaser.
- End with a clean reservation invitation, not a generic sales claim.

## What Not To Do

- Do not invent engine, efficiency, price, warranty, or alternator specifications.
- Do not reveal the product image too early; preserve the unveiling narrative.
- Do not use bright consumer-tech gradients or playful motion.
- Do not make the CTA imply the product is already commercially released.
