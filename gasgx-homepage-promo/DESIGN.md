# Design System

## Overview

GasGx homepage promo: a dark energy intelligence platform with a neon green data signal. The visual language combines a rotating world map, country rankings, compact navigation taxonomy, and technical dashboard surfaces. The promo should feel like a premium energy-market operating system: precise, global, analytical, and field-ready.

## Colors

- **Canvas**: `#050505` — near-black starfield and page background.
- **Panel**: `#0F0F0F` — navigation, cards, and footer surfaces.
- **Deep Panel**: `#080C0A` — green-tinted depth behind technical overlays.
- **Primary Text**: `#FFFFFF` — main claims and product names.
- **Muted Text**: `#94A3B8` — supporting descriptions and metadata.
- **Accent Green**: `#5DD62C` — data signals, rankings, CTA emphasis, and map highlights.
- **Restricted Red**: `#EF4444` — use sparingly for map status contrast only.

## Typography

- **Primary**: Inter, weights 300-900.
- **Technical labels**: JetBrains Mono, weights 400 and 700.
- **Display**: Inter 900 with normal letter spacing.
- **Metrics**: JetBrains Mono 700 for country counts and rankings.

## Motion

- Use slow orbital movement, map zooms, scanning rules, and measured signal pulses.
- Use green data routes as visual transitions between beats.
- Keep the final CTA calm and high-contrast rather than flashy.

## Components

- **Global map hero**: captured homepage globe as a live-looking world intelligence surface.
- **Ranking metric**: compact `25+ COUNTRIES` badge with mono numerals.
- **Operating system panel**: tools, products, use cases, and rankings grouped into structured rows.
- **Brand lockup**: GasGx plus the three-part promise: unstrand, reinvent, monetize.

## What Not To Do

- Do not use bright full-screen gradients or generic SaaS illustration.
- Do not turn the dense navigation taxonomy into unreadable tiny text.
- Do not introduce unrelated blue, purple, or warm consumer palettes.
- Do not imply unsupported performance claims beyond the captured homepage copy.
- Do not let captions cover the main globe or core CTA content.
