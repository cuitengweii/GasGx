Controls, gas filtration and usage notes

  1. Controls cover power switch, manual/automatic start-stop, breaker control, operating data display and fault history.
  2. Protection includes overspeed, underspeed, over/under voltage, over/under frequency, overcurrent, overload, low oil pressure, high water temperature, high exhaust temperature and battery voltage alarms.
  3. Recommended gas filtration: H2S <200mg/Nm³, particulate content <30mg/Nm³, particle diameter <5μm, water content <20g/Nm³ and gas temperature <40℃.
  4. The unit must be properly grounded on site, installed with sufficient ventilation, connected through inspected gas flanges, and maintained with dedicated gas-engine oil and suitable antifreeze coolant.
