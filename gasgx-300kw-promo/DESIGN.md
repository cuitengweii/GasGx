# Design System

## Overview

GasGx G300 uses a dark industrial product interface with a technical sales-sheet feel. The page combines full-width generator photography, neon green instrumentation accents, compact stat cards, and blueprint-style engineering diagrams. Layouts are dense but orderly: hero proof cards, image directory panels, specification grids, and delivery-scope checklists. The identity should feel precise, engineered, and field-ready rather than glossy consumer tech.

## Colors

- **Primary Surface**: `#090909` — main black canvas.
- **Panel Surface**: `#141414` — card and specification panel background.
- **Deep Surface**: `#080C0A` — green-tinted black for industrial depth.
- **Text Primary**: `#FFFFFF` — high-contrast product headings.
- **Text Bright**: `#F8F8F8` — primary UI copy and brand text.
- **Text Muted**: `#CBD5E1` — body copy and secondary labels.
- **Text Quiet**: `#94A3B8` — navigation and small metadata.
- **Border Soft**: `#202020` — quiet dividers and panel edges.
- **Accent Green**: `#5DD62C` — CTAs, stat emphasis, glow, diagram trace highlights.
- **Spec Gray**: `#9CA3AF` — small supporting labels.

## Typography

- **Primary Font**: Inter, weights 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900.
- **Display**: Inter 900, about 72px on the source page, tight technical presence with normal letter spacing.
- **Section Heading**: Inter 400, about 40px, clean and restrained for engineering sections.
- **Body**: Inter 300, about 18px with generous line height, used for descriptive product copy.
- **Labels and Stats**: Inter 800-900 for numbers and unit labels; use tabular numerals for counters.
- **Icon Font**: Font Awesome appears in navigation, but promo compositions should favor captured SVGs and custom line work over icon fonts.

## Elevation

Depth comes from thin borders, tinted black panels, and localized neon-green glows rather than large drop shadows. Cards often use `#141414` with 1px green or white-low-opacity borders, subtle inset highlights, and radial green bloom near important metrics. Product imagery sits inside dark frames with restrained green outlines, creating the feeling of an equipment dashboard.

## Components

- **Industrial Hero Panel**: Dark split hero with a large G300 title, concise product copy, and a neon green quote button.
- **Quote + Proof Card**: Compact card showing `36800$`, `300KW`, `37%`, and `8000h` with green accents and tabular numerals.
- **Generator Photo Frame**: Full-width product exterior image with black surround, green frame details, and equipment-console atmosphere.
- **Blueprint Directory**: Engineering diagram thumbnails with numbered list navigation and thin neon outlines.
- **Specification Grid**: Dense dark cards for model, speed, voltage, efficiency, weight, and other parameters.
- **Delivery Scope Checklist**: Two-column configuration panel with standard factory configuration and optional modules.
- **Green CTA Button**: `#5DD62C` fill, dark text, 8px radius, subtle green glow.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Use `#5DD62C` as the only dominant accent and let it drive glows, counters, rules, and CTA emphasis.
- Keep backgrounds dark, technical, and layered with radial glows, grid lines, and blueprint traces.
- Use captured generator and diagram assets prominently; the product photo is the signature visual.
- Use dense but readable specification panels with clear numeric hierarchy.
- Keep card radius near 8px and borders thin.

### Don'ts

- Do not use bright full-screen gradients or unrelated tech palettes.
- Do not make the video text-only; every beat should carry product imagery or engineering diagrams.
- Do not over-round cards or buttons beyond the source style except for small pills.
- Do not introduce playful illustrations, soft lifestyle imagery, or consumer SaaS styling.
- Do not replace the captured Inter typography with generic web fonts.
